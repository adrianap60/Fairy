the Golden Fairy

This new character will represent memories, warmth, happiness, and the memories that stay with you even as time passes. Ella es la idea de que "even if tomorrow I'm not here, the memories i left behind are still alive."

She will have long soft golden-blonde hair, with soft flowy wavy hair like the other two fairies. Similar face as the other two faries aswell. Her outfit would be a flowy yellow/gold dress with sparkles. She will have hoop earrings with a small star-shaped necklace. And her wings would have this golden soft glow.
